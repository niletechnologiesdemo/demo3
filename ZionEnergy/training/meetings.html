01  BOX SWELL | 7.14.5.6
Place a straightedge lengthwise along the box OD. If there is a visible gap, use calipers to compare the OD at the bevel with the OD 2 inches (± 1/2 inch) away. Reject the connection when the OD at the bevel is larger by 1/32 inch or more.

02  SHOULDER FLATNESS | 7.14.5.15
On the box, place a straightedge across a diameter of the face and rotate it at least 180 degrees in the shoulder plane. Repeat on the pin with the straightedge across a chord of the shoulder. Any visible gap between the straightedge and shoulder is cause for rejection.

03  BOX OUTSIDE DIAMETER | 7.16.4(a)
Measure box OD 4 inches (± 1/4 inch) from the shoulder. Take at least two measurements spaced 90 degrees (± 10 degrees) apart. Apply the appropriate acceptance criteria: manufacturer OD requirements; Table 7.38 for components connected to HWDP; or customer-specified bending strength ratio (BSR), using box OD with pin ID, for drill collars or similar components. Manufacturer criteria govern conflicts.

04  PIN STRESS RELIEF GROOVE (SRG) | 7.16.4(e)
Unless waived by the customer, API connections NC38 and larger that make up to BHA components, including applicable saver-sub connections, require a pin SRG; reject when the required feature is absent. Measure groove diameter and width against Table 7.37 or 7.38. Measure groove length from the connection shoulder to the first full thread, with a metal rule on the thread taper and squared against the shoulder.

05  MAGNETIC FIELD MAGNITUDE / ORIENTATION | 7.14.4(e)
Verify the field under blacklight with a magnetic flux indicator strip or magnetic penetrameter on the internal connection surface while solution is being applied and power is activated. Perform at the start of each inspection, after every 25 connections, whenever component diameter, thickness or length changes, when the inspector changes, on request, and upon completion.
