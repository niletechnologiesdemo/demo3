--------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO OPEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Unzip the folder anywhere, then double click index.html.
It opens in any browser. No internet connection is needed: the fonts
are bundled inside the folder, so the wireframes look identical in a
meeting room with no wifi.

Best viewed at a browser width of 1600 pixels or more. Two screens,
Rate Master and QMS, have wide tables that scroll sideways on a
narrower window.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS IN HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
index.html    The screen index. Start here. Every screen in the
              consolidated platform, grouped by area, each tagged
              EXISTING or NEW MODULE and carrying the release number
              from the proposal.

pages/        46 screens. Click any card on the index to open one.
              Every screen carries the same left hand navigation, so
              you can walk the whole application from any starting
              point. "Screen Index" in the sidebar returns here.

css/          One stylesheet shared by every screen.
fonts/        Barlow, Barlow Condensed and JetBrains Mono, bundled.
js/           Navigation, modal and toast behaviour.
_source/      The earlier single file source and the Hunter mobile
              user flow document, kept for reference only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THE TAGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
EXISTING      Already delivered in CrewViewPro. Shown so the client
              sees the whole application, but not rebuilt and not
              re-billed in this proposal.

NEW MODULE    Built during the fusion. These screens carry a gold
              banner at the top explaining what the module does and
              why it sits where it does.

R1 to R5      The release in which the screen is delivered, matching
              section 9 of the consolidation proposal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SUGGESTED WALKTHROUGH FOR THE CLIENT MEETING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  index.html            One application, 46 screens, half of it
                          already built.
2.  Dashboard             The single home screen.
3.  Inspection Requests   A client raises a request.
4.  Create Job            It becomes a dispatched inspection job,
                          using the crew and scheduling you already
                          have.
5.  Inspection Detail     The stage bar across the top is the whole
                          story: dispatched, checked in, items
                          received, inspection, submitted, approved,
                          report issued, invoiced.
6.  Inspection Tickets    What physically arrived at the yard.
7.  Crew View Capture     The inspector's phone. Offline, with the
                          form generated from the item template and
                          tolerances checked as the reading is typed.
8.  Item Summary          The result, its evidence, and the price
                          and margin for that single item.
9.  Rate Master           Where price comes from, and the cost column
                          that makes margin measurable.
10. Invoice Detail        Items, inspector hours and mileage priced
                          automatically. Nothing billed at zero.
11. Profitability         Quoted against delivered, per inspection.
12. Client Portal View    What the client sees. Same platform,
                          separate audience, no cost or margin.
