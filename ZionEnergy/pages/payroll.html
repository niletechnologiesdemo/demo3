Where the hours came from
Nothing on this screen is keyed in. Regular and overtime hours come from geofenced check-in and check-out at the customer site. Mileage comes from the trip log. Deductions come from the employee record. The same hours that pay the crew are the hours that cost the job — so payroll and job profitability can never disagree.
