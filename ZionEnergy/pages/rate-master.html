How a Price Is Chosen
1. Purchase Order line
If the client has issued a PO with agreed rates, that rate wins and the PO balance is drawn down.
2. Client specific override
A negotiated rate for this client or location, without changing the standard catalogue.
3. Standard catalogue price
The default price from this table, effective dated and versioned.
4. No match
The item is flagged unpriced and appears on an exceptions list. It is never silently invoiced at zero.
