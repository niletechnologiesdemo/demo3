Access Rules
Portal users can
Raise and track inspection requests · view and download approved reports · view invoices, statements and payment status · upload or reference a purchase order · see the status of their own items.
Portal users cannot
See Zion internal cost or margin · see employee records, attendance or payroll · see another client's data · see draft or unapproved inspections · see the rate master cost column.
Invite Portal User
Creates a scoped client login
Portal roles are a separate tree from staff roles. A portal user can never inherit a Zion internal permission.
