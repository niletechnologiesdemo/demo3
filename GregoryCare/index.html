Companionship When You Need it Most

Families book non-medical home care for themselves or a loved one. Gregory Care schedules the visits, the caregiver clocks in and out, and the family receives a report after every visit.

How a booking flows

No quotes or approvals — a request comes in, the owner schedules it, and the family can see everything.

1Client

Books care for themselves or a family member — one-time, weekly or picked dates

2Owner

Confirms the visits and assigns a caregiver — usually "Me"

3Client

Sees every visit, its time and the caregiver in the app

4Caregiver

Clocks in on arrival — the family sees "here now"

5Caregiver

Clocks out and writes the visit report

6Owner

Reviews the report (office concerns stay internal) and shares it

7Client

Reads the report — tasks, ADLs, mood, meals and a summary

8Owner

Hours from clock in/out roll up into each caregiver's timesheet

9Owner

Adds caregivers as the business grows — they get an SMS invite

10Family

Messages Gregory Care about any booking, any time
